# Little Wins — brand guide

## Positioning
- Generic parenting-help account for US/UK/AU parents, so it can sell any product (Robo Coder book now, apps later).
- IG name field example: "Little Wins | Helping Parents Help Their Kids Grow".
- New account → no link in bio yet; CTA = DM / comment a keyword (e.g. comment WANT).
- Address the viewer as "you / your kid / parent" — never "mom/mum" only, so dads feel included. No first-person narrator ("I") on the brand account.

## Structure (Duolingo model)
- Mascot = permanent brand face + one fixed voice (warm female, 30–40, medium-low pitch) for tips and the closing line of every video.
- Story videos use separate human characters / narrators.

## Mascot (concept C "big head")
- Blue mother bear `#4F6FD0`, lighter face mask, cream muzzle.
- Big round THICK navy glasses `#1F2A5C`.
- Playful fur TUFT curling up (a tuft — not a bun, not a hat).
- Cub: sunny yellow `#F6C453` with its own tuft (family trait).
- Files: `brand-little-wins/maskot/` (`konsep-C-kepala-besar.png` = reference).

## US ad characters
- Mom: honey-brown wavy hair, freckles, coral cardigan `#EE7A5F` over a white tee.
- Boy, 7: sandy-blond with a tuft, freckles, gap teeth, red tee `#E5483B` with white trim.
- Pose sheets: `brand-little-wins/karakter-us/`.

## Script & video rules
- Script pattern: "You're a parent, and you have a problem…" → cause → step-by-step → real result → product reveal → CTA (see `skills/membuat-video-iklan-2d/references/naskah-masalah-solusi.md`).
- Visual style: bright 2D storybook, one bright focal point, background muted; AI images must contrast with the background (hex colors in prompts).
- Production workflow, research bank and scripts: `skills/membuat-video-iklan-2d/`.
