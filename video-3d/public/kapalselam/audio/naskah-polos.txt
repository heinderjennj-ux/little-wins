You're a fisherman... and you have a problem. Someone just offered you FIVE billion dollars to build a boat that sinks... and comes back up. But you only get paid if your crew comes back up too, and all you've ever built are fishing boats, boats that float. But you REALLY want a jet-powered speedboat... So HERE'S how you get the money!
First, you just push your fishing boat underwater. Five minutes, tops. It pops right back up. Boats REALLY like floating. So you build big tanks along the sides, and let seawater flood in. Now the boat is heavy enough to sink. These are called ballast tanks. But now it sinks... and sinks... and won't stop. So you add small tanks you can fill or empty a little at a time, until the boat hangs perfectly still, halfway down. These are called trim tanks. But every thirty-three feet you go down, the water presses harder. Deep enough, and your boat crumples like a soda can. So you build the inside as a thick steel tube, because a round shape spreads the pressure evenly. This is called a pressure hull. You pack a few air tanks. Enough for a whole weekend, you think. Your crew breathes through them before lunch. So you run electricity through water, and split it into oxygen. This is called electrolysis. But every breath out fills the boat with carbon dioxide, and your crew gets dizzy. So you pump the air through a chemical that soaks it up. This is called a CO2 scrubber. And down here, it's pitch black. So you send out a ping of sound, and listen for the echo. This is called sonar. Then, deep in the dark... a pipe bursts. Seawater blasts in. The boat gets heavier... and starts sinking past its safe depth. The walls start to groan. If it keeps going, you lose the boat, the crew, and every single dollar. You sit on the cold floor, in the red emergency light... and you think this is the end. And then... you remember. Every time you pulled a fish up from deep water too fast, it came up puffed like a balloon. The air inside it grew bigger... and shot it to the surface. So you grab the emergency air, the strongest you've got, and blast it ALL into the ballast tanks at once. The water is forced out. And as you rise, the air expands even more, pushing you up faster... and faster. This is called an emergency blow.
And your submarine LEAPS out of the ocean like a whale! Your crew is safe. You get your five billion dollars, and you buy the jet-powered speedboat. Just one problem: you're now terrified of water. So the speedboat lives in your living room.
